# Research Index

This directory contains compiled research on AI agents and related topics.

## Files

### 01-manus-ai-research.md
Comprehensive research on Manus AI - a general-purpose AI agent platform. Covers product features, architecture, pricing, use cases, user reviews, and technical implementation details.

### 02-russian-ai-agents-market.md
Overview of 31 AI agent services available in the Russian market. Includes categorization by type (assistant apps, bot platforms, specialized tools), pricing models, and market analysis.

### 03-international-ai-services.md
Research on international AI aggregator services including GPTunnel, GoGPT, BotHub, and ChadGPT. Covers features, pricing, and user feedback for multi-model AI access platforms.

### 04-ai-agents-reviews-criticism.md
Compilation of user reviews, criticism, and community discussions about AI agents from Reddit, Product Hunt, and Habr. Includes practical concerns and user experiences.

### 05-development-cases.md
Case studies and practical development stories including "Mila" agent development story and local AI agent implementation challenges.

### 06-russian-llm-saas.md
Research on Russian LLM services including YandexGPT, GigaChat, and Bitrix24 integration. Covers features, limitations, and enterprise use cases.

### 07-mobile-ai-apps.md
Comprehensive research on mobile AI assistant applications from Google Play Store. Covers official apps (ChatGPT, Copilot), multi-model aggregators (Genie, ChatOn, TalkAI), and specialized apps with ratings, features, and user feedback analysis.

### 08-cis-ai-agents-usage.md
Research on AI agent adoption across CIS region (Russia, Kazakhstan, Belarus, Ukraine). Covers marketplace sellers, bloggers, freelancers, and SMB segments with usage statistics, popular tools, challenges, and implementation recommendations.

### 09-freelance-ai-market.md
Analysis of AI services market on freelance platforms (FL.ru, VC.ru). Contains 60+ project requests, typical budgets, skill requirements, Telegram AI bot market research, and pricing trends for AI-related freelance work.
