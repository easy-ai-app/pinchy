### 13-russian-business-ai.md

## Key Insights

### AI Agents Market
- High hype but limited production use
- Works well for narrow, defined workflows
- Fails on open-ended tasks
- Quality depends heavily on data and prompts

### Russian Market Specifics
- Payment via SBP, RUB cards, Telegram Stars
- Strong demand for Russian-language solutions
- GigaChat and YandexGPT lead local market
- Telegram bots as primary AI access point

### Mobile Apps Trends
- Aggregation of multiple models in one app
- Telegram Mini Apps gaining popularity
- Voice control and multimodality standard
- Free tier limited to 10-20 requests/day
